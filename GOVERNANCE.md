1. Purpose
The Treasury Governance Framework defines the authority, structure, responsibilities, and accountability mechanisms governing the beast-system-3-treasury repository.
It ensures that all treasury operations—on‑chain and off‑chain—are executed with:

constitutional integrity

financial transparency

multi‑sig security

audit‑ready documentation

lawful compliance

For deeper constitutional authority, see Treasury Charter.

2. Governance Principles
The Treasury Division operates under the following mandatory principles:

Sovereign Accountability — all actions traceable to authorized roles

Least Privilege — minimal access required for duties

Separation of Powers — governance, operations, and security remain distinct

Transparency — all financial actions logged and auditable

Security First — multi‑sig, key rotation, and immutable logs

Compliance Alignment — federal filings and DAO law upheld

For security specifics, see Security Policy.

3. Governance Roles & Authorities
3.1 Chief Executive Officer (CEO)
Authority Level: Sovereign
Responsibilities:

Final authority over treasury governance

Approves or vetoes treasury policies

Can invoke Emergency Freeze

Oversees LUCR token supply governance

Signs federal filings and compliance documents

See Emergency Freeze Protocol.

3.2 Treasury Governance Officer (TGO)
Authority Level: High
Responsibilities:

Maintains treasury governance documents

Oversees spending authority matrix

Ensures compliance with internal controls

Coordinates audits and reporting cycles

See Spending Authority Matrix.

3.3 Treasury Operations Officer (TOO)
Authority Level: Operational
Responsibilities:

Executes approved treasury transactions

Maintains transaction ledger

Performs monthly reconciliations

Manages program funding workflows

See Treasury Register.

3.4 Security Officer (SO)
Authority Level: Critical
Responsibilities:

Manages key rotation

Oversees access control

Responds to security incidents

Validates smart contract integrity

See Key Management Policy.

3.5 Compliance Officer (CO)
Authority Level: Regulatory
Responsibilities:

Maintains federal filings

Oversees risk assessments

Ensures audit readiness

Maintains reporting calendar

See Risk Assessment.
