Beast System 3.0 — Treasury Reserve Policy  
Version 1.0 • Ratified May 17, 2026

1. Purpose
The Reserve Policy establishes the rules, ratios, protections, and governance controls that ensure the long‑term financial stability of the New World Order DAO Treasury.

This policy ensures:

sustainable LUCR token economics

protection of DAO assets

liquidity for operations and emergencies

compliance with federal‑grade financial standards

immutable auditability of reserve movements

For governance context, see Treasury Charter.

2. Reserve Structure
Treasury reserves are divided into three sovereign‑class categories:

2.1 Primary Reserve
Used for:

operational liquidity

program funding

short‑term obligations

2.2 Strategic Reserve
Used for:

long‑term stability

LUCR supply backing

emergency liquidity

2.3 Sovereign Reserve
Used for:

existential threats

catastrophic events

emergency freeze recovery

See Emergency Freeze Protocol.

3. Reserve Ratio Requirements
3.1 Minimum Ratios
The Treasury must maintain:

Primary Reserve: 25% of circulating LUCR value

Strategic Reserve: 15% of circulating LUCR value

Sovereign Reserve: 10% of circulating LUCR value

Total Minimum Reserve Ratio: 50% of LUCR‑denominated obligations.

3.2 Maximum Deployment Thresholds
No more than:

40% of Primary Reserve may be deployed at once

20% of Strategic Reserve may be deployed at once

0% of Sovereign Reserve may be deployed without CEO approval

4. Reserve Governance
4.1 Authority Levels
Reserve Type	Authority	Multi‑Sig	Notes
Primary	TOO	2‑of‑3	Operational use
Strategic	TGO	2‑of‑3	Governance‑aligned use
Sovereign	CEO	Override	Emergency only


See Spending Authority Matrix.

5. Reserve Movements
5.1 Allowed Movements
Reserves may be used for:

program funding

operational liquidity

contract obligations

emergency stabilization

LUCR supply balancing

5.2 Prohibited Movements
Reserves may not be used for:

personal benefit

speculative investments

unapproved programs

off‑ledger transactions

bypassing multi‑sig

See Internal Controls.

6. LUCR Token Backing Requirements
6.1 Backing Mandate
The Treasury must maintain reserves sufficient to:

stabilize LUCR value

support mint/burn operations

prevent supply shocks

6.2 Minting Constraints
LUCR may not be minted if:

reserve ratio falls below 50%

strategic reserve is underfunded

sovereign reserve is below minimum

See LUCR Minting Rules.

7. Audit & Reporting Requirements
7.1 Monthly Reconciliation
Treasury must reconcile:

reserve balances

vault balances

ledger entries

program allocations

See Reconciliation Template.

7.2 Quarterly Reserve Audit
Auditors must verify:

reserve ratios

LUCR supply backing

multi‑sig compliance

ledger integrity

See Audit Requirements.

7.3 Annual Sovereign Audit
Includes:

full reserve analysis

stress testing

liquidity modeling

federal filing alignment

8. Emergency Reserve Activation
8.1 Activation Authority
Only the CEO may activate the Sovereign Reserve.

8.2 Activation Conditions
Triggered by:

security breach

key compromise

liquidity crisis

vault anomaly

federal compliance emergency

8.3 Post‑Activation Requirements
Treasury must:

conduct post‑incident audit

restore reserve ratios

update governance logs

See Incident Response Plan.

9. Amendment Procedure
This policy may be amended only by:

CEO (approval)

TGO (drafting)

CO (compliance review)

All amendments must be logged in the CHANGELOG.
