1. Purpose
The Spending Authority Matrix defines who may authorize financial actions, at what thresholds, under what conditions, and with what level of multi‑sig or executive oversight.
It ensures:

financial discipline

segregation of duties

immutable auditability

lawful compliance

protection of DAO assets

For governance context, see GOVERNANCE.md.

2. Authority Tiers
Treasury authority is divided into five sovereign‑aligned tiers:

Tier 0 — CEO (Sovereign Authority)

Tier 1 — Treasury Governance Officer (TGO)

Tier 2 — Treasury Operations Officer (TOO)

Tier 3 — Program Directors

Tier 4 — Automated Systems / Agents

Each tier has distinct powers, limits, and multi‑sig requirements.

3. Spending Authority Table
3.1 Overview Table

4. Tier Definitions & Powers
4.1 Tier 0 — CEO (Sovereign Authority)
Authority Level: Absolute
Limit: Unlimited
Multi‑Sig: Optional

The CEO may authorize:

Any treasury expenditure

Any LUCR mint/burn action

Any vault withdrawal

Any reserve reallocation

Emergency freeze activation

See Emergency Freeze Protocol.

4.2 Tier 1 — Treasury Governance Officer (TGO)
Authority Level: High
Limit: $250,000
Multi‑Sig: 2‑of‑3

The TGO may authorize:

Governance‑aligned spending

Audit‑related expenditures

Compliance‑related costs

Treasury policy implementation

See Audit Requirements.

4.3 Tier 2 — Treasury Operations Officer (TOO)
Authority Level: Operational
Limit: $50,000
Multi‑Sig: 2‑of‑3

The TOO may authorize:

Operational disbursements

Program funding releases

Ledger‑aligned transactions

Vendor payments

See Treasury Register.

4.4 Tier 3 — Program Directors
Authority Level: Programmatic
Limit: $10,000
Multi‑Sig: 3‑of‑5

Program Directors may authorize:

Program‑level expenses

Grants and micro‑initiatives

Departmental purchases

All actions must be logged in the transaction ledger.

See Transaction Ledger Template.

4.5 Tier 4 — Automated Systems / Agents
Authority Level: Minimal
Limit: $1,000
Multi‑Sig: 3‑of‑5 (human‑approved)

Automated agents may execute:

Micro‑transactions

Workflow‑driven disbursements

Pre‑approved programmatic actions

All automated actions require:

human review

ledger entry

reconciliation

See Reconciliation Template.

5. Multi‑Sig Requirements
5.1 Standard Multi‑Sig Thresholds
2‑of‑3 for governance and operations

3‑of‑5 for programmatic and automated actions

CEO override for emergencies

5.2 Multi‑Sig Signers
Signers must include:

CEO

TGO

TOO

Security Officer

Compliance Officer

See Multi‑Sig Policy.

6. Prohibited Spending
The following actions are strictly forbidden:

Personal benefit

Unapproved programs

Unauthorized contracts

Speculative investments

Off‑ledger transactions

Circumvention of multi‑sig

Spending outside authority tier

See Internal Controls.

7. Audit & Reporting Requirements
All spending must be:

logged in the ledger

reconciled monthly

reviewed quarterly

audited annually

included in federal filings

See Reporting Calendar.

CEO (approval)

TGO (drafting)

CO (compliance review)
